PATIENT QUESTIONS

Before Your Consultation

A few simple points to help patients and parents prepare.

What can I discuss during a consultation?

You can discuss skin, hair, scalp and child-care concerns, symptoms, relevant history and questions about ongoing care.

Can parents discuss a child's skin concern?

Yes. Parents or caregivers can bring questions about common childhood concerns and pediatric skin conditions for discussion and assessment.

Should I bring previous prescriptions or reports?

If you have relevant previous prescriptions, reports or a list of medicines, keeping them available can help provide useful context during the consultation.

How do I request an appointment?

Use the appointment page or contact the clinic directly by phone or WhatsApp for appointment information.
