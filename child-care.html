CHILD CARE SERVICES

Healthy childhood. Confident parents.

Caring for a child comes with many questions, from the first days at home to the teenage years. At Dr. Shivani Nivedita's Skin & Child Care Clinic, every consultation is unhurried and parent-friendly. Concerns are listened to carefully, explained in simple language, and followed by practical guidance and follow-up support wherever needed.

01

Newborn Care

The first weeks and months with a new baby are full of joy, and also full of questions. Newborn care consultations give parents a calm space to discuss how their baby is feeding, sleeping, gaining weight and settling into a routine. Newborn skin is delicate and can show many harmless but worrying changes, so parents can also ask about rashes, dryness, peeling, jaundice and everyday skin care.

Each baby is assessed individually, and parents receive clear, practical advice they can follow confidently at home.

You can discuss:
  • Breastfeeding and feeding questions
  • Sleep patterns and settling
  • Weight gain and general wellbeing
  • Newborn jaundice
  • Newborn skin concerns such as rashes, dryness and cradle cap
  • Cord care, bathing and everyday handling
02

Child Health

Children can fall ill often, especially in their early years, and it is not always easy for parents to know what is routine and what needs attention. Child health consultations focus on understanding your child's symptoms, history and day-to-day routine, followed by a careful clinical assessment.

Parents are given a clear explanation of what is happening, what to do at home, which precautions to take and when a follow-up review is needed.

You can discuss:
  • Fever, cough and cold
  • Stomach upset, loose motions and vomiting
  • Recurring or persistent symptoms
  • Appetite, eating habits and general health questions
  • Frequent illnesses and low energy
  • Guidance on care at home
03

Growth & Development

Every child grows at their own pace, but parents naturally want to be sure their child is on the right track. Growth and development consultations look at your child's height, weight and developmental milestones in the context of their age, health and family background.

If you have noticed slow growth, delayed milestones or changes in behaviour or feeding, this is the place to talk it through. Concerns are assessed carefully, and parents are guided on the appropriate next steps.

You can discuss:
  • Height and weight concerns
  • Developmental milestones such as sitting, walking and talking
  • Speech and language development
  • Feeding and nutrition questions
  • Behaviour, sleep and daily routine
  • Concerns about slow or unusual growth
04

Adolescent Care

The teenage years bring physical, emotional and social changes, and many teenagers and parents are unsure where to turn with their questions. Adolescent care consultations offer age-appropriate, respectful guidance for health, skin and wellbeing concerns during puberty.

Teenagers are given space to ask questions openly, and parents receive clear guidance on how best to support them.

You can discuss:
  • Acne, oily skin and other skin changes during puberty
  • Hair and scalp concerns
  • General health and wellbeing
  • Changes related to growth and puberty
  • Concerns about energy, sleep and daily habits
  • Questions from teenagers and parents
05

Childhood Allergies

Allergies can affect a child's comfort, sleep and daily life. They may show up as sneezing, a runny or blocked nose, itchy eyes, skin rashes, itching or discomfort after certain foods, dust or seasonal changes. Childhood allergy consultations help parents understand recurring symptoms and what may be triggering them.

After a careful history and assessment, parents receive practical advice on managing symptoms, reducing exposure to triggers and knowing when to seek further review.

You can discuss:
  • Recurring sneezing, blocked nose and itchy eyes
  • Skin allergies and rashes
  • Reactions after certain foods
  • Dust, seasonal and environmental triggers
  • Symptoms that keep returning or worsen at night
  • Everyday steps to reduce triggers at home
06

Childhood Infections

Infections are a common part of childhood, but persistent or recurring symptoms can worry any parent. Childhood infection consultations help assess symptoms carefully, understand how long they have been present and decide on appropriate care.

Parents receive clear guidance on care at home, hygiene and precautions, and when a follow-up is needed, so that they feel informed and reassured throughout their child's recovery.

You can discuss:
  • Fever and infections of the throat, chest or ear
  • Stomach and gut infections
  • Skin infections, including fungal infections
  • Symptoms that are persistent, recurring or concerning
  • Hygiene and precautions at home and school
  • Follow-up and recovery guidance
07

Pediatric Skin Conditions

A child's skin is delicate and different from an adult's, so skin concerns in children need age-appropriate care. Pediatric skin consultations cover common conditions affecting newborns, children and adolescents, with treatment and advice suited to your child's age and skin type.

Parents are given simple, practical guidance on skin care routines, triggers to avoid and how to manage the condition day to day.

You can discuss:
  • Eczema and dry, itchy skin
  • Diaper rash and skin irritation in infants
  • Fungal and other common skin infections
  • Rashes, allergies and reactions
  • Acne in adolescents
  • Everyday skin care, bathing and product choices
08

Infant & Everyday Care

Parenting brings up small questions every day, and they deserve clear, simple answers. Infant and everyday care consultations are a chance to talk through routine child-care matters that may not feel serious enough for a full visit, but still leave parents unsure.

The aim is to help parents feel confident with the daily care of their child through practical, easy-to-follow guidance.

You can discuss:
  • Bathing, skin care and hair care
  • Feeding routines and introducing new foods
  • Sleep habits and daily routine
  • Clothing, hygiene and general care
  • Common everyday concerns and habits
  • Anything else you would like to understand better as a parent
