CHILD CARE SERVICES

Healthy childhood. Confident parents.

Caring for a child comes with many questions, from the first days at home to the teenage years. At Dr. Shivani Nivedita's Skin & Child Care Clinic, every consultation is unhurried and parent-friendly. Our child care services bring paediatric consultation, neonatal care, lactation support, vaccination guidance, paediatric dermatology, adolescent care and allergy and asthma support together in one place.

CHILD & ADOLESCENT CARE

Our Child Care Services

Dedicated services for newborns, children, adolescents and parents, with a focus on clear communication, practical guidance and age-appropriate care.

01

Paediatrician

Comprehensive paediatric consultation for common childhood health concerns, symptoms, growth, nutrition and everyday questions from parents and caregivers.

02

Neonatal Care

Parent-friendly support during the newborn period, including feeding, weight gain, newborn wellbeing, common newborn concerns and early-life care questions.

03

Lactation Counselling Centre

Support and counselling for breastfeeding concerns, feeding patterns, latch-related questions, milk supply concerns and building confidence during the early months.

04

Vaccination Centre

Vaccination-focused consultations to help parents understand recommended immunisations, schedules, catch-up questions and practical preparation for visits.

05

Paediatric Dermatology

Age-appropriate evaluation and guidance for common skin concerns in newborns, children and adolescents, including rashes, eczema, irritation and acne.

06

Adolescent Clinic

A respectful space for teenagers and parents to discuss puberty, skin changes, hair and scalp concerns, general wellbeing and healthy daily habits.

07

Breast Feeding Support

Practical support for parents who want help with breastfeeding routines, feeding confidence, common challenges and continuing breastfeeding comfortably.

08

Allergy & Asthma Clinic

Assessment and guidance for recurring allergy or breathing-related concerns, including triggers, symptoms, home precautions and when follow-up may be appropriate.
